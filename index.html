FAQ

Questions clients ask

How long does it take?

About 5 weeks from our first call to launch. Bigger systems take longer, and you get the timeline in writing before we start.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and your content and customer data belong to you.

What if you disappear?

Your domain and data are in your name, and we sign an agreement before any work starts, so you're never locked out of your own site.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, updates, backups, and small edits like new photos, prices, or text.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. You get up to two rounds of changes on the preview before launch, then small edits each month.

How do I pay?

In milestones, never 100% upfront. Payments go only to my real name, after each step is done.
